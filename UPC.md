## 📋 Metadata Standards

### UPC/EAN Format
- Standard: EAN-13 (13-digit barcode)
- Issuing Authority: GS1 Global
- Registration: UnitedMasters & CL40 World

### Catalog ID Format
- Format: Alphanumeric (QZ prefix for variations, QZZEB for album tracks)
- Purpose: Internal tracking and rights administration
- Authority: CL40 World LLC
